Tidy Trees in 3D

Each family of siblings is separated along x or z by the unchanged 2D contour walk, and centred under its parent along the other axis. Every layout here is computed by space::layout3 in the crate, checked pair by pair for overlapping boxes, and reproduced bit for bit by the flat engine.
